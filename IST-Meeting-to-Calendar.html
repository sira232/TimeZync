IST Meeting to Calendar

Enter the meeting exactly as given (India time). It converts to your phone's local timezone and creates a ready-to-save calendar event.

Open Google Calendar Download ICS

IST is a fixed UTC+5:30 offset (no daylight saving), so the conversion is always exact — including rolling the date backward/forward when needed. The local time shown comes from your phone's own clock/timezone. For Outlook, open the downloaded .ics file and add it (works with the Outlook app or your phone's default calendar). Tap Reset to clear the form and start the next meeting. Twin of IST-Meeting-to-Calendar.hta (Windows-only).
