TimeZync

Enter the meeting exactly as given in its own timezone. It converts to your phone's local timezone and creates a ready-to-save calendar event. Tap ⚙️ to add more titles or timezones.

Open Google Calendar Download ICS

Each timezone above is a fixed offset (no daylight saving), so the conversion is always exact — including rolling the date backward/forward when needed. The local time shown comes from your phone's own clock/timezone automatically; you never need to set that yourself. For Outlook, open the downloaded .ics file and add it (works with the Outlook app or your phone's default calendar). Tap Reset to clear the form and start the next meeting. Twin of IST-Meeting-to-Calendar.hta (Windows-only).
